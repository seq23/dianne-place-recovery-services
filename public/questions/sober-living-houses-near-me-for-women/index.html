Frequently asked questions

What is the difference between detox and sober living?

Detox provides medical management for acute withdrawal symptoms under clinical supervision. Sober living offers a substance-free, structured residential environment for individuals who have already stabilized and want peer support during early recovery.

Do I need to attend recovery meetings while living in the house?

Yes, maintaining active participation in community support meetings or mutual aid groups is a standard expectation. This practice strengthens personal accountability and connects you with a broader network of peers navigating similar experiences.

Can family members visit residents in the home?

Yes, visitors are generally welcome during designated daytime hours according to household policies. These guidelines ensure that visits respect the privacy, comfort, and quiet time of all other women sharing the residence.

What should I bring when moving into a sober living home?

Bring comfortable clothing, personal hygiene items, bed linens, and any prescribed medications in their original containers. House staff provide a complete checklist of allowed items prior to your scheduled move-in day.
